# ReadLite 隐私政策
ReadLite 不收集、不存储、不传输任何用户个人数据。所有设置（字体、背景色等偏好）仅保存在用户本地浏览器中。本插件不包含任何追踪代码或第三方分析服务。
最后更新：2026年3月

# ReadLite Privacy Policy
ReadLite does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data.
All settings (font, background color, and other preferences) are saved locally in the user's browser only.
This extension contains no tracking code or third-party analytics services.
Last updated: March 2026
